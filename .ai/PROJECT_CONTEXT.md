# Project Context — Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya Website

## What Is This Project?

A production website for **Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya**, a music and dance academy in Gurugram, India. The site serves as the academy's primary online presence — showcasing courses, enabling enquiries, and building credibility.

- **Production URL**: https://tansensangeetgurugram.com
- **Repository**: `tansen_v1`
- **Version**: 0.1.0 (active development)

## Business Goal

Drive **free demo class bookings** and **course enquiries** from parents and students in Gurugram. Every page should funnel visitors toward the `Book Free Demo` or `Enquire Now` CTAs.

## Target Audience

| Segment | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Parents (primary) | Parents of children aged 3-16 looking for extracurricular activities |
| Students (secondary) | Young adults and adults wanting to learn music/dance |
| Career seekers | People looking for certified music/dance education |

- Premium, educational, artistic, trustworthy, modern, warm, professional
- NOT a generic children's activity website
- Course pages follow a unified template system — only content changes per course
- Dark navy heroes with gold accents, cream/white content areas
- Mobile-first responsive design
