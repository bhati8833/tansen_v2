# Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya — Official Website Content Document

## About Us

**Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya**, a recognized leader and premier music & dance school, is a proud unit of **Tansen Institute of Performing Arts (TIPA) Pvt. Ltd.** Beyond providing high-quality training, the institution is deeply committed to a social mission — making performing arts accessible to people across the nation.

Founded in memory of the late **Sh. M. L. Verma**, the academy continues to grow under the guidance of his sons: **Madan Lal Verma, L. S. Verma, S. K. Verma, and K. G. Verma**. While the elder two have established their own enterprises, the younger two—along with their spouses—are actively involved in managing and expanding the institution’s vision.

They are ably supported by a dedicated team including **Mr. Ravi Kumar Verma, Mr. D. K. Verma, Mr. V. K. Verma, and Mr. Puneet Verma**, who help steer Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya as a dynamic and inclusive platform for learning music and dance.

## Our Courses

At **Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya**, we believe that every child has a unique creative potential waiting to be discovered. Our programs are designed to nurture musical and artistic abilities through structured learning, expert guidance, and regular practice.

From Indian classical traditions to contemporary Western arts, students get an opportunity to explore their interests, build confidence, develop discipline, and express themselves creatively. Whether they are beginners or looking to refine their skills, our courses offer a supportive environment for learning and growth.

---

### 1. Classical Vocal Singing

Our Classical Vocal Singing program introduces students to the rich and timeless tradition of Indian classical music. Through structured training and regular practice, students develop their voice, sense of rhythm, pitch, and musical expression. Whether you are a beginner exploring singing for the first time or an experienced learner aiming to refine your vocal skills, the academy provides personalized guidance for every stage of learning.

#### What You Will Learn
- Fundamentals of Indian Classical Music
- Understanding and practice of Swar (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni)
- Alankar and basic vocal exercises
- Introduction to Ragas and their characteristics
- Understanding Taal and Laya (rhythm and tempo)
- Voice culture, breathing techniques, and vocal control
- Practice of Aroh, Avroh and Pakad of different Ragas
- Bollywood & Light Music
- Developing pitch accuracy, rhythm, and listening skills
- Stage performance, confidence, and musical expression

---

### 2. Kathak

Discover rhythm, graceful movement, expressions, traditional compositions, and storytelling through Kathak. Our Kathak program introduces students to the rich heritage of Indian classical dance through structured training.

#### What You Will Learn
- Fundamentals and history of Kathak
- Basic Tatkar and rhythmic footwork
- Mudras and Hastaks (hand gestures)
- Understanding Taal, Laya, and rhythm
- Basic Kathak compositions and sequences
- Chakkars (spins) and graceful movements
- Abhinaya and facial expressions
- Learning traditional Tukdas, Todas, and Tihais
- Coordination, posture, balance, and body control
- Stage performance, confidence, and presentation
- Appreciation of Indian culture and classical dance traditions

---

### 3. Western Dance

Our Western Dance program is designed to help students explore movement, rhythm, and self-expression in a fun and energetic environment. Through structured training, students learn a variety of Western dance techniques while developing coordination, flexibility, musicality, and confidence. Whether you are stepping onto the dance floor for the first time or refining advanced moves, our academy provides personalized guidance at every stage of your learning journey.

#### What You Will Learn
- Fundamentals of Western Dance
- Basic dance positions, movements, and techniques
- Body coordination, balance, and flexibility
- Understanding of rhythm, beats, and musicality
- Choreography, routine performance, and freestyle expression

### 4. Guitar

Our Guitar program is designed to help students build a strong foundation in guitar playing through a combination of practical training and music theory. The course encourages regular practice and creativity, helping students gain confidence in playing both independently and as part of a group. Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya has been nurturing musicians through structured training, experienced faculty, and a student-focused learning approach.

#### What You Will Learn
##### Acoustic Guitar Playing
- Correct posture, hand positioning, and playing techniques
- Basic chords and chord progressions
- Strumming patterns and rhythm techniques
- Playing melodies, riffs, and simple songs
##### Rhythm & Timing
- Basic music theory and scales
- Finger exercises and coordination techniques
- Developing rhythm, timing, and musicality
- Introduction to different genres and playing styles

### 5. Keyboard / Piano

Our Keyboard and Piano program introduces students to the fundamentals of playing while building a strong understanding of music. Through structured lessons and regular practice, students develop hand coordination, rhythm, pitch, and musical expression. The course is designed for beginners as well as students looking to strengthen their existing skills, with learning progressing at an individual pace.

#### What You Will Learn
- Introduction to the Keyboard/Piano and its fundamentals
- Correct posture, hand position, and finger techniques
- Understanding musical notes and notation
- Learning scales, chords, and arpeggios
- Basic music theory and rhythm
- Developing hand coordination and finger strength
- Reading and playing simple musical pieces
- Understanding melody, harmony, and chord progressions
- Playing songs across different musical styles
- Improving timing, expression, and musicality
- Building confidence through performance and practice

---

### 6. Drums

Our Drums program introduces students to the fundamentals of drumming in an energetic and engaging learning environment. Students develop rhythm, coordination, timing, and control while learning essential drumming techniques and patterns. The course combines structured practice with practical playing, helping students build confidence and a strong musical foundation.

#### What You Will Learn
- Introduction to the Drum Kit and its components
- Correct posture, grip, and basic playing techniques
- Fundamental drum beats and rhythmic patterns
- Understanding tempo, timing, and rhythm
- Basic drum notation and music theory
- Coordination between hands and feet
- Stage Performance Skills
- Playing along with songs and different musical styles
- Developing speed, control, and rhythmic accuracy
- Building confidence through individual and group performances

---

### 7. Tabla

Our Tabla program introduces students to the rich rhythmic traditions of Indian classical music through systematic and progressive training. Students learn the fundamentals of Tabla playing while developing rhythm, coordination, concentration, and musical understanding. The course combines traditional techniques with regular practice, helping students build a strong foundation and confidence in performing.

#### What You Will Learn
- Introduction to the Tabla and its structure
- Correct sitting posture, hand positioning, and playing techniques
- Basic Bols and their combinations
- Understanding Taal, Laya, and rhythm
- Practice of fundamental Taals and rhythmic patterns
- Developing hand coordination and finger control
- Improving speed, clarity, timing, and rhythmic accuracy
- Building confidence through individual and group performances

---

### 8. Fine Arts

Our Fine Arts program provides students with a creative space to explore their imagination and express themselves through art. Through guided practice, students learn fundamental artistic techniques while developing observation, creativity, patience, and attention to detail. The course encourages students to experiment with different mediums and develop their own unique artistic style.

#### What You Will Learn
- Fundamentals of Drawing and Sketching
- Understanding shapes, lines, forms, and proportions
- Colour theory and colour mixing
- Introduction to Painting and different mediums
- Shading, highlighting, and creating depth
- Still life and object drawing
- Nature, scenery, and creative compositions
- Exploring creativity through freehand and imaginative art

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. What age group can join Tansen Sangeet Mahavidyalaya?
We welcome learners across different age groups, from young children (3+) to adults. Programs and teaching methods are adapted according to the student’s age, level, and learning ability.

### 2. Do you offer classes for beginners?
Yes. Our programs are suitable for both beginners and students with prior experience. No previous training is required to get started.

### 3. Which courses do you offer?
We offer training in Classical Vocal Singing, Kathak, Western Dance, Guitar, Keyboard/Piano, Drums, Tabla, and Fine Arts.

### 4. Can I attend a demo class before enrolling?
Yes, students can enquire about a demo class before choosing a program. We provide a free of cost demo class. This gives them an opportunity to experience the class and understand the learning process.

### 5. Do students need to bring their own instruments?
For instrument classes such as Guitar, Keyboard/Piano, Drums, and Tabla, students may be advised to have access to the respective instrument for regular practice. Our team can guide you regarding the requirements.

### 6. How often are the classes conducted?
Classes are conducted on regular basis. You are required to complete minimum 2hrs in a week. The exact days and timings may vary depending on the program, age group, and batch availability.

### 7. Will students get opportunities to perform on stage?
Yes. Students are encouraged to participate in performances, events, and cultural programs, giving them valuable experience and helping build confidence and stage presence.

### 8. Can students learn more than one course?
Yes, students can explore multiple disciplines depending on their interests and the availability of suitable batches.

### 9. How can I enquire about admissions and fees?
You can contact us through Phone, WhatsApp, email, or our online enquiry form. Our team will be happy to provide information about admissions, available batches, course fees, and schedules.

### 10. Are the courses and programs affiliated with any universities or organizations?
Yes, we are affiliated with major UGC accredited universities and renowned music societies in India, such as **Prayag Sangeet Samiti**. It is also associated with **Trinity College, London**, offering globally recognized certification programs in music and performing arts. These affiliations ensure that students receive quality education and can pursue performing arts as a serious career option.
